Our Story

A Passion for
Working with Wood.

AS
Arun Sadashiv SutarFounder & Owner · Vishwakarma Furniture Works

My name is Arun Sadashiv Sutar. I have been working in the woodworking and furniture industry for 20 years. What started as a passion for working with wood has grown into Vishwakarma Furniture Works.

Over the years, I have worked on different types of wooden projects, including doors, chokhat, furniture and decorative wood carving. I also specialize in CNC wood carving and customized wooden designs.

At our workshop in Bahireshwar, every project receives attention to detail. Customers are welcome to visit, discuss their ideas and understand the work before placing an order.
